  <what-built>
    Phase 23 delivered, all automated-tested against mocks and all green:

    - `PresentationViewer.vue` — a Teleport-to-body, pure-black, full-screen viewer that walks the
      flat `assembledSlideshow` array. Fullscreen API on open with a fixed-overlay fallback when the
      promise rejects; `fullscreenchange` synced to the component's own exit. Keyboard navigation
      scoped to the viewer root (→/Space next, ←/Backspace prev, Esc exit), stop-at-ends with no
      wrap. Auto-hiding bottom chrome bar (progress pill `{section} · N / M`, prev/next chevrons
      disabled at the ends) plus a top-right exit button, both fading after ~3s idle. Projection-scale
      rendering for lyric, copyright, scripture, congregational scripture, text and image slides;
      loading and empty states with a reachable exit in both.
    - `AudioPlayer.vue` / `VideoPlayer.vue` — a new `chromeless` prop (no native scrubber, no panel
      chrome, no internal affordance button, video sized to 80vh), plus a new exposed `isMuted`
      accessor and `unmute()` on `VideoPlayer`.
    - Imperative media driving — the outgoing slide's media is paused before every index change,
      the incoming slide's media plays after it mounts, and playback stops on exit and unmount.
    - Three degraded states — the gray "Media unavailable" notice, the centered "Tap to play
      audio"/"Tap to play video" affordances, and the amber "Playing muted — tap to unmute" chip.
    - The "Present Slideshow" CTA in the `SlideshowPreview` panel header, disabled with an
      explanatory title when there is nothing to present, wired through `ServiceEditorView`.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Start the app with `npm run dev` and open a service that has songs, scripture and at least one
    PPTX-imported deck. Attach an MP3 to one slot and an MP4 to another (Phase 22 media attachment
    UI, in the slot rows).

    **1 — Entry and fullscreen (R016).**
    a. Scroll to the Slideshow Preview panel on the Music tab. Confirm a "Present Slideshow" button
       sits in the panel header next to the title.
    b. On a service with no songs or scripture, confirm the button is greyed out and hovering it
       shows "Add songs or scripture to build a slideshow to present."
    c. Click it on a populated service. Expect: true browser fullscreen, pure-black canvas, the
       first slide centered and large.
    d. Press the browser's OWN Esc-to-exit-fullscreen. Expect: the viewer closes completely and you
       are back in the editor — NOT dark full-screen chrome stranded in a normal-sized window.
    e. Click "Present Slideshow" again. Expect: it opens normally a second time.

    **2 — Navigation (R016).**
    a. Press → and Space repeatedly to the last slide. Expect: the next chevron greys out and further
       presses do nothing — the show does not wrap back to slide 1.
    b. Press ← and Backspace back to the first slide. Expect: the prev chevron greys out, no wrap.
    c. Confirm the progress pill reads like "Worship · 3 / 24" on sectioned slides.
    d. Click the on-screen chevrons and the × button with the mouse. Expect: they work identically to
       the keys. On a touch device, confirm all three are comfortably tappable.
    e. Confirm every slide type looks right: lyric lines, the song copyright/title slide, scripture,
       a congregational reading with Leader/Congregation styling, an imported text slide, an image.

    **3 — Media playback (R016, C-2/C-3).**
    a. Advance onto the slide with the attached MP3. Expect: audio starts on its own, or a centered
       "Tap to play audio" button appears if your browser blocked it.
    b. Advance onto the MP4 slide. Expect: the video plays large and centered; if the browser only
       allowed a muted start you should see the amber "Playing muted — tap to unmute" chip — tap it
       and confirm sound comes on. If the browser blocked it entirely you should see "Tap to play
       video" instead.
    c. HOLD the → key down through a run of slides that includes both media slides. Expect: NO
       lingering audio or video from a skipped slide playing underneath the current one. This is the
       flagged rapid-key-repeat case — listen carefully.
    d. Confirm nothing loops: let the video run to its end and confirm it stops.

    **4 — Graceful degradation (R016, C-1).**
    a. Delete the attached media file from Firebase Storage (or point a slot at a URL you have since
       removed) so the slide's URL is dead, then present that slide.
    b. Expect: a small gray "Media unavailable" note in the lower-left area, the slide's own text or
       image still rendering normally, and → still advancing. No broken media icon, no stuck spinner,
       no halt.

    **5 — Chrome and legibility (R018).**
    a. Stop touching the mouse and keyboard for ~3 seconds. Expect: the bottom bar and the × button
       fade out; the slide content does not.
    b. Move the mouse. Expect: they fade straight back in.
    c. Confirm the "Media unavailable" note stays visible while the chrome is faded.
    d. Put it on the actual projector or a large TV. Read the smallest projected text from the back
       of the room. Confirm the CCLI copyright/license lines are fully present and not cut off.

    **6 — The two flagged overflow cases (judgment call).**
    a. Present the longest text slide from the `docs/example.pptx` import. Does the 48px body text
       overflow or clip? Nothing shrinks it automatically — that was deliberately not built.
    b. Present a congregational reading with as many Leader/Congregation exchanges as you have.
       Does the stack run off the bottom?
    c. Tell us whether either is bad enough to warrant a follow-up shrink-to-fit or scroll task.
       "Fine as-is" is a perfectly good answer and closes both flags.

    **7 — iPad/iPhone Safari (if available).**
    Open the same service on an iPad or iPhone in Safari and press "Present Slideshow". Expect: a
    usable full-viewport presentation — either true fullscreen or the black overlay fallback. Both
    are correct outcomes; only "it does not open at all" is a defect.
  </how-to-verify>
