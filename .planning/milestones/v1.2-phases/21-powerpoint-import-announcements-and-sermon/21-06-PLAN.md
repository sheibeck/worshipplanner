## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-21-06-01 | Tampering | IMPORTED slot bound to a foreign importId | low | mitigate | The slot only stores an importId; deck reads go through the org-scoped importedSlides store + firestore.rules, so a cross-org importId cannot be read. |
| T-21-06-02 | Repudiation / Data loss | PC-export mishandling an IMPORTED slot (Pitfall 2) | low | mitigate | Explicitly skip IMPORTED in the (slot as any) export branches, verified during the end-to-end checkpoint; no silent mislabel. |
| T-21-06-03 | Denial of Service (cost) | source .pptx never deleted | low | accept | Locked CONTEXT tradeoff; retention handled by Phase 22; the checkpoint confirms the file persists (never deleted by this phase). |
