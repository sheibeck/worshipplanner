<objective>
Land the two small, pure, foundational additions to `functions/src/index.ts` that BOTH Phase 61 triggers build
on: (1) add `'lock-notification'` to the `MessageType` union + the `MESSAGE_TYPES` validation array so the
`queueServiceMessage` callable accepts the lock email, and (2) two exported pure date helpers —
`todayInTimeZone(tz, now)` and `minusDays(ymd, n)` — that compute the org-local calendar date for the R145
reminder-due check with no new dependency.

Purpose: R144 send-path plumbing (the enum gate at `functions/src/index.ts:987` rejects `'lock-notification'`
until it is in `MESSAGE_TYPES` — this is the whole reason 61-04's client enqueue can reach a write) and the
R145 / R133 org-local date primitives the 61-02 reminder cron needs. Keeping these two pure, unit-tested
additions in their own Wave-1 plan lets 61-04 (client lock hook) depend only on the tiny type change and lets
the timezone math be proven in isolation — the single most error-prone part of R145 (research Pitfall 3) — before
any `onSchedule` scan exists to obscure a boundary bug.

DECISION (locked, from 61-RESEARCH.md § Timezone Feasibility): compute org-local "today" with
`Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-CA', { timeZone })` — NO date library. Node 22 ships full ICU so any IANA zone
resolves, and `en-CA` emits `YYYY-MM-DD` directly; the in-repo `formatServiceDate` (`:1120-1130`) already relies
on `toLocaleDateString({ timeZone })`, so this is precedent, not a new capability.

Output: `'lock-notification'` in `MessageType`/`MESSAGE_TYPES`, the exported `todayInTimeZone`/`minusDays`
helpers, and their unit tests in `functions/src/index.test.ts`. No package installed, nothing deployed.
</objective>
