<objective>
Ship the dedicated re-lock notify modal `src/components/ReLockNotifyPrompt.vue` (approved 62-UI-SPEC, 9
sub-components) — NOT the free-text `MessageComposer`, because the notice content IS the auto-generated diff. It
lists the typed CHECKABLE `ChangeEntry` rows with type badges + affected-team chips, offers an affected-teams
(default) vs everyone recipient choice with a live "Reaches N", a primary "Send notice" that enqueues a
`relock-notification`, and an always-available "Lock quietly" — reusing the `PptxImportModal` shell and the
composer's `resolveRecipients`/Reaches-N/`queueServiceMessage` idioms.

Purpose: R146 (the scoped typed checkable diff surface) + R147 (each entry team-tagged; affected-vs-everyone
choice) + R148 ("Lock quietly" always available). The modal owns the send + the recipient math; the snapshot
overwrite (SC4) is the parent hook's job (62-04) — this modal signals it by emitting `sent` (send succeeded) or
`cancel` (Lock quietly / dismiss), which 62-04 both map to the overwrite.

DECISIONS (locked, from 62-UI-SPEC.md / 62-CONTEXT.md / 62-RESEARCH.md / 62-PATTERNS.md — do NOT re-litigate):
- Dedicated modal in the `PptxImportModal.vue` `<Teleport>`+backdrop+`Transition` shell (`max-w-lg`), reusing the
  shipped checkbox recipe (`ServiceEditorView.vue:510-514`), the Phase 60 muted type-badge idiom, the
  `TeamTagPill` chip recipe, and the composer's Reaches-N footer.
- All rows DEFAULT CHECKED; "Affected teams" is the DEFAULT recipient choice (R147 primary intent) resolving to
  the union of affectedTeams across CHECKED entries; "Everyone on this service" is the opt-up.
- Reaches-N recomputes on every check/uncheck and choice switch via `resolveRecipients`. No individual-person
  picker — `individualPersonIds` is always `[]`.
- "Lock quietly" is ALWAYS enabled (SC3). ✕ / backdrop / Escape = quiet-lock semantics (emit `cancel`). On send
  FAILURE the modal stays open, shows an inline red error, re-enables Send, and emits nothing (the parent must
  NOT overwrite the snapshot on a failed send — SC4 safe retry). No success toast — the modal closes on success
  (the failure-only ToastHost would misrender a success string; the Phase 60 history panel is the durable record).
- Send is disabled when zero entries are checked OR the selection reaches zero people OR a send is in flight; it
  carries a `title` explaining the block (62-RESEARCH § Open Question 2).

Output: `src/components/ReLockNotifyPrompt.vue` + `src/components/__tests__/ReLockNotifyPrompt.test.ts`. No
functions/ change (62-01 already landed the wire), no snapshot write (62-04's job), nothing deployed.
</objective>
