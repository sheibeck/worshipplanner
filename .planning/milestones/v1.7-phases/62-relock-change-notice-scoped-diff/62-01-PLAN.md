`'relock-notification'` to the `MessageType` union and the `MESSAGE_TYPES` validation array so the

the owner deploys later — NO new Function, NO new secret, NO new index, NO deploy.

  `:1208-1211` already anticipates this exact add. Touch nothing at the `:1372` gate.

  `changeDiff?: ChangeEntry[] | null` to `QueueMessageRequest` (`:1249-1259`); destructure it in the handler
