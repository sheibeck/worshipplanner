<objective>
Restore store-as-source-of-truth for the three components the review flagged as bypassing it: a
direct org-doc write + hand-sync in ServiceTemplateEditor (R355/ARCH-007), and two component-local
onSnapshot listeners with no owning store — GettingStarted's member count and ConfigurationTab's
super-admins list (R356/ARCH-008).

Purpose: Move writes and listener lifecycles behind stores so the subscribe/unsubscribe and
write+sync machinery is owned in one place, per the codebase's documented pattern.
Output: A new auth-store settings mutation, two small owning stores, and the three components rewired
to consume them — behavior-preserving, app suite green.
</objective>
