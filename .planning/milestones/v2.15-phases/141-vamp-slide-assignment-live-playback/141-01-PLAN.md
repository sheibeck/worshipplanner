**Noun that is primary:** unchanged — the output-role model (audience / confidence / video) and the
per-slide audio slot (`GroupSlideEntry.audioUrl`).
**Decision:** `no-change`.
**Rationale:** Both terms are pre-existing wording, not a new architectural plural. Multi-monitor output
roles shipped in v2.9 (Phase 114) and v2.14 (Phase 137, the third "Video" role); this phase adds no
fourth role and no per-output setting (CONTEXT.md lists per-output audio routing as explicitly deferred).
"Choose a Vamp" is an ordinary user selection that writes into the ONE existing per-slide audio slot —
`vampId`/`vampLabel` are display-only annotations of that slot, not a second audio source. The one
genuine identity change this phase makes — audio ownership moving from the output windows to the Run
control window — is a locked CONTEXT.md owner decision (2026-09-13), not a singular→plural transition,
and is implemented in Plans 141-02/141-03 by removing the outputs as audio owners (promote-by-removal),
not by adding a second owner alongside.
</assumption_delta_decision>
