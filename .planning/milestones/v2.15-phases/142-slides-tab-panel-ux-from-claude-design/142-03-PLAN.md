<objective>
Create `SlideGroupSetupStrip.vue` — the new thin composing wrapper that IS variant 7a: one row of three
chips (Display · Background · Audio), each stating its current value and opening its own small
absolutely-positioned popover that hosts the finished control from Wave 1 (`SlotVideoOutputControl`
tiles, `BackgroundControl variant="chip-popover"`, reworked `SlideGroupMusicControl`). The strip owns only
UI state (`openChip`, `alignRight`, listener lifecycle, focus) and bubbles six passthrough emits — it never
touches a store.

Decision areas implemented (142-CONTEXT.md): "Layout variant & panel structure" (three chips + popovers,
one open at a time, click-outside/Esc, locked = visible-but-inert, Tailwind tokens not mockup hex) and
the chip-state half of "Background & Display chips" / "Audio slot" (Add-on-dashed, '{file} (song)', amber
'♪ … · no MP3', row-end caption). Claude's-discretion items resolved here: popover is inline
`position:absolute` (no Teleport — UI-SPEC §2 explains the panel sits above the grid's scroll region),
widths 232px / 258px (audio), focus-on-open + return-on-Esc, document `pointerdown` click-outside.

Purpose: Plan 04 swaps this component into `SlideGrid.vue`'s panel; it must be complete and tested in
isolation first so the integration is a wiring change, not a build.
