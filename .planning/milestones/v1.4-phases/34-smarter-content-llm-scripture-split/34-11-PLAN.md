<objective>
Put group music and group background in one panel.

**This is not R064 work.** It is owner UAT finding F2 from the 2026-08-03 hands-on session
(`34-UAT.md`), a Phase 33 layout finding planned here because it surfaced in the same session.

Purpose: `SlideGrid.vue` renders them as two sibling rows, each in its own wrapper `div` — the music
control at `:73-82` and the background control at `:90-102`, added by 33-08 as *"a NEW sibling row
directly below the music control."* Both are group-level media on the same `canWriteGroupMedia` gate.
Nothing prevented one panel; they were simply built as separate rows, and the owner reads them as two
unrelated sections.

Output: one panel containing both, with every existing condition, caption and handler intact.

## ★ Phase 36 owns the broader rework of this surface — keep this small

Phase 36 ("UI Rework — Service Order & Contextual Action Bars", R067/R068/R069/R053) rebuilds this
area against the Claude Design wireframes and moves group-level actions into a contextual action bar.
This plan is deliberately **not** that rework: it merges two adjacent wrappers into one and changes
nothing else, so it cannot conflict meaningfully with whatever Phase 36 does to the same region. No new
component file, no shared panel abstraction, no restyle of the controls themselves, no relocation out
of `SlideGrid.vue`. If a change here starts to feel like design work, it belongs to Phase 36.

## This is a layout change. No behaviour may change.

Four things are load-bearing and are individually asserted rather than assumed:

1. **Each control's own wrapper `v-if` semantics.** `31-UI-SPEC E5` is cited twice in this file
   (`:68-72` and `:84-89`): *"gate the WRAPPER, not just the control's contents"*, because a control
   with nothing to show and no add affordance renders an empty bordered box, *"which a locked service
   hits constantly."* Those two conditions survive verbatim as named computeds, and the merged panel
   gets the same treatment one level up — **the panel must not render empty either.**
2. **The `canWriteGroupMedia` gate**, including its deliberate song-group carve-out. The file's own
   comment at `:84-89` insists background uses the SAME gate as bed audio and *"never
   `canMutateGroup`"*. Merging must not blur the two.
3. **The caption at `:451-453`** — the Copywriting Contract's group-background caption with the real
   card count substituted (R055). The owner quoted it in the finding; it survives word for word.
4. **`songBackgroundForInheritedDisplay` (`:463-470`)** — populated only for a SONG group whose own
   background is empty while the song's is set, derived from already-resolved provenance on the
   group's own assembled slides, *"never from a new song-lyrics prop or a second cascade derivation."*
