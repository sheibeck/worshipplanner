## ★ Premise correction verified at plan time

CONTEXT.md describes `src/stores/slideGroups.ts` (~line 213) as holding "the scope-handling branch"
and RESEARCH.md calls it "the ONE read path". **Verified against live source during planning: it is a
doc-comment paragraph, not executable code.** A whole-`src/` grep finds the identifier in exactly
four places — the type declaration, two lines inside that `/** ... */` block in the store, and three
sites in the drawer (two write routes plus the reset initializer), and the test fixtures. There is
**no executable reader anywhere in the codebase**. The field is write-only today.

This makes the deletion safer than the research claimed, not riskier — but the plan must say so
plainly, because "delete the branch at :213" would otherwise send an executor looking for code that
does not exist. What goes at that location is the stale paragraph describing behaviour that will no
longer be true.
