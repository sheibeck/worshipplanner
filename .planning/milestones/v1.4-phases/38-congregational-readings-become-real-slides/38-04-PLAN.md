  <behavior>
    - Helper: a function that applies one rebuild tick the way the composable does — run `rebuildGroup`, and when it reports a change, write both the returned slides AND the freshly computed `sourceSignature` back onto the group, exactly as the apply loop does. Every case below drives state through this helper, never by assigning slides directly.
    - CONVERT then IDLE: a Reference-state group on a slot given three sections converts to three section entries on tick one and reports no change on ticks two and three.
    - DELETE ONE STAYS DELETED: after conversion, removing the middle entry and running two further ticks leaves exactly two entries, in order, carrying the first and third sections' speakers and words. The removed section's words appear nowhere in the group.
    - DELETE ALL STAYS DELETED: after conversion, removing every entry and running two further ticks leaves an empty group.
    - EDIT SURVIVES: after conversion, changing one entry's stored words and running two further ticks leaves that entry carrying the changed words and every sibling byte-identical.
    - SPEAKER FLIP SURVIVES: the same, for a changed speaker.
    - REORDER SURVIVES: after conversion, reversing the entries and running two further ticks leaves them in the reversed order.
    - DESTROY ON REFERENCE CHANGE: after conversion, moving the slot to a different passage via `scriptureSlotAfterReferenceChange` and running two ticks leaves exactly ONE entry whose `sourceRef` deep-equals the payload-free scripture ref, and assembling that group yields exactly one slide showing the NEW reference with an empty passage body.
    - DESTROY ON CLEARED REFERENCE: after conversion, clearing the slot's reference via the same function and running two ticks leaves no scripture entry, while a hand-added video entry appended before the clear is still present.
    - RE-CONVERT: after a destroy-by-reference-change, giving the new slot three sections and running a tick converts again to three section entries — the owner must opt back in, and opting back in must work.
    - RE-SPLIT: after conversion, replacing the slot's sections with two different ones and running two ticks yields exactly two entries carrying the new sections — never five, never a growing list.
    - MIGRATION, congregational: a group in the shape a Phase-34 service actually holds today — one payload-free scripture entry, `sourceSignature` equal to the bare formatted reference — on a slot that already carries three sections, converts to three section entries on tick one and reports no change on tick two. Assembling it yields three slides.
    - MIGRATION, non-regression: a group in that same stored shape on a slot with NO sections reports no change on the very first tick. Deploying this phase must not rewrite a single ordinary scripture group.
    - PROJECTED OUTPUT: after conversion, `assembleSlideshow` over the group yields one slide per surviving entry, each carrying its own speaker and words, in group order.
  </behavior>
  <action>
Create `src/utils/__tests__/congregationalDetachment.test.ts`. It is a sibling to
`congregationalReadingPipeline.test.ts`, not a replacement: that file asserts the composed contract
at one instant, this one asserts it holds across repeated rebuild ticks. Say that in the head
comment, and say why — a rebuild that reverts the user's work does so on a LATER reactive tick, and
a test that runs the rebuild once cannot see it.

Build the tick helper first, and model it on how the composable actually applies a rebuild outcome:
read that code before writing the helper. If the helper writes the slides but not the refreshed
signature, every case below will pass for the wrong reason — the group would look permanently stale
and be re-derived forever, which is the exact failure mode the cases are meant to catch. Assert the
helper's own fidelity with a case that converts and then reports no change on the following tick.

Reuse the fixture technique the sibling file established: one source passage genuinely partitioned
into sections, with a repeated speaker in the fixture so the same-speaker case is covered by the
fixture every other case uses rather than by a special one, plus the non-ASCII backstop.

Every state change to the SLOT must go through `scriptureSlotAfterReferenceChange`. Hand-building a
slot with a changed reference and intact sections would test a shape production cannot reach, and
would quietly hide the fact that clearing sections on a reference change is what makes the destroy
branch fire at all.

Every state change to the GROUP's entries — a deletion, an edit, a reorder — must be expressed as
the array a real caller would write, then fed through the tick helper. Do not call the rebuild's
internals directly.

Run two further ticks after each mutation, not one. A one-tick assertion cannot distinguish "this
holds" from "this holds until the next recompute".
