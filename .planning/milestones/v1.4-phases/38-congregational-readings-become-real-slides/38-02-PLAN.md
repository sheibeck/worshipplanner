<objective>
Make "one slide, one section" true in the type system and on the projector: replace
`ScriptureSlide.sections?: CongregationalSection[]` with a singular
`ScriptureSlide.section?: CongregationalSection`, and rework the projected scripture slide so the
speaker sits on its own line above that section's passage text.

Delivers roadmap criterion 2 and closes the CONTEXT discretion question about the now-unused
multi-section rendering branch: it is DELETED in this phase, not left standing.

Purpose: an array field invites a future change that stacks sections back onto one slide. A
singular field makes that impossible to express, and makes every test assertion about the new
shape positive rather than an absence check.
Output: the projector renders exactly what the owner asked for — speaker on top, scripture below.
</objective>
