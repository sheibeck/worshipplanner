<objective>
Strip the confirm/review SURFACE — every component, prop, store action and test that
exists only to ask a human whether a slide group may update (R048). Consumers first,
orphaned file last, exactly as Phase 27 sequenced its three deletions.

Purpose: this is the consumers-first half of R048. Doing it first makes
`ReconcileConfirmModal.vue` provably orphaned before it is deleted, and leaves every
remaining plan free of prop-chain churn.

**Deliberately NOT in this plan — do not do it here:** the pure rebuild engine
(`slideGroupMaterializer.ts`), the composable's decide/apply loop
(`useSlideshowAssembly.ts`), and the `SlideGroup` type field. Those move together in
30-02 because deleting the engine's confirm gate is only safe in the same change that
generalizes hand-added-slide survival to SCRIPTURE and IMPORTED groups. Removing this
UI first cannot lose data: the engine still returns `changed: false` for a
confirm-required divergence, so nothing is written.

Known, accepted transient between this plan and 30-02: a customized scripture/imported
group whose source has diverged keeps its stale slides with no affordance to resolve
them. That is the pre-existing stall with its banner removed, not new data loss, and
30-02 resolves it.

Output: a Slides tab with no reconciliation UI, a green type-check, and a smaller prop
surface for 30-02 to land against.
</objective>
