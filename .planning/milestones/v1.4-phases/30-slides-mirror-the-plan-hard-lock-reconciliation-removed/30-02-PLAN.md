<objective>
Replace the three-branch reconciler with ONE unconditional, idempotent rebuild per slot
kind, generalize hand-added-slide survival from SONG to SCRIPTURE and IMPORTED, narrow
scripture derivation to a single reference-only slide, and rename `reconcile*` to
`rebuild*` (R046, R047, R048).

Purpose: this plan is the phase's load-bearing risk. Today only SONG's additive merge
preserves a user's dropped video or authored text. SCRIPTURE and IMPORTED groups are
protected ONLY because the confirm gate stalls. 30-01 removed the gate's UI; this plan
removes the gate itself. If the survival guarantee is not generalized in the SAME change
that deletes `hasCustomization`, this phase silently destroys user slides with no
dialog — regressing Phase 24 D-02 on the very phase that removes the dialog. Task 2
does both together, in that order, deliberately.

The second risk is idempotence. Two prior defects live in this exact function — 28-03
compounded a twice-referenced chorus 2 to 4 to 8 to 16 by re-emitting a whole stored
array per occurrence, and 26-09 silently dropped a duplicated entry by keying stored
entries one-per-section. Both are the same failure class: a rebuild that is not order-
and multiplicity-preserving. After this plan EVERY path is the additive path, so both
fixes are preserved verbatim and idempotence is ASSERTED for N=M, N<M and N>M, not assumed.

Output: a pure rebuild layer whose correctness comes from idempotence rather than from a
change-detection gate, and a composable whose only decision is "write, or don't bother".
</objective>
