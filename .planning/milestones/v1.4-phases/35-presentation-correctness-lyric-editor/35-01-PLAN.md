R059 and R061 — the two presentation-layer corrections that both land in `PresentationViewer.vue`.

**R061** threads the Slides-tab selection through to the viewer so presenting starts where the
user is looking.

mid-deck must open on the slide the user had highlighted.

tests that pin all four.

**backgrounds** in `PresentationViewer.vue` are **OUT OF SCOPE** for Phase 35 (35-UI-SPEC.md §6).

R059/R060/R061/R065/R066 mentions backgrounds, and adding compositing here would turn a
correctness phase into a rendering feature with its own contrast/legibility/scrim design work.
**Add no markup, no prop, and no `background-image` CSS to `PresentationViewer.vue`.**
