1. **The panel wrapper** (line 81-85): `<div v-if="showGroupMusicControl || showGroupBackgroundControl" class="mx-6 mt-3 flex flex-col gap-3 rounded-md border border-gray-800 bg-gray-900 px-3 py-2" data-testid="slide-grid-group-media-panel">`
2. **The music control** (line 93-102): `<SlideGroupMusicControl v-if="showGroupMusicControl" ... flush ... />` — a DIRECT child of the panel, therefore itself a flex item.
3. **The background testid wrapper** (line 119-132): `<div v-if="showGroupBackgroundControl" data-testid="slide-grid-group-background">` containing `<BackgroundControl ... flush ... />`. The WRAPPER is the flex item; `BackgroundControl`'s own root is one level deeper.

**Why classes land where they land.** Both child components' roots are `<div :class="flush ? '' : 'rounded-md border border-gray-800 bg-gray-900 px-3 py-2'">`. Under `flush` the bound class evaluates to the empty string, so a `class` attribute placed on the `<SlideGroupMusicControl>` TAG falls through and merges cleanly onto its root div — verified in both component sources. That is why the music control needs no wrapper of its own.

`BackgroundControl` is different: it is NOT the flex item, its wrapper is. The wrapper exists solely because `data-testid="slide-grid-group-background"` cannot move onto the component (`BackgroundControl`'s root already carries `data-testid="background-control"`, which would collide). Keep the wrapper; give the WRAPPER the flex-item classes. Putting them on `<BackgroundControl>` instead would style a grandchild and do nothing to the row.

**Existing assertions that constrain the edit** (`SlideGrid.test.ts`, describe block `'group media panel (34-11 Task 1)'`, ~lines 856-928):
- Panel classes MUST still contain `rounded-md`, `border`, `border-gray-800`, `bg-gray-900`, `px-3`, `py-2`.
- Panel classes MUST still NOT contain `divide-y` or `divide-gray-800`.
- `panel.findAll('.border-gray-800').length === 0` and `panel.findAll('.bg-gray-900').length === 0` — zero DESCENDANTS may carry panel chrome.
- Zero descendants may carry `px-3` AND `py-2` together.
- Render matrix: both controls / music only / background only / no panel at all, across editor and viewer.
- `html.indexOf('slide-group-music-control') < html.indexOf('background-control')` — music stays first in source order.

None of the new classes (`flex-wrap`, `items-start`, `gap-x-6`, `gap-y-3`, `min-w-[14rem]`, `flex-1`) collide with any of those. No existing test asserts `flex-col` or `gap-3` on the panel — verified by grep — so removing them breaks nothing.

**Test harness facts** (already present in `SlideGrid.test.ts`, do not re-create): `mountGrid({ selectedSlot, group, isEditor, ... })`, `makeSlot({ kind, id, position })`, `makeGroup({ bedAudioUrl, backgroundImageUrl, slides })`. `SlideGroupMusicControl` and `BackgroundControl` are already imported at the top of the file.

**Tailwind is v4** (`tailwindcss: ^4.0.0`). Arbitrary values such as `min-w-[14rem]` are supported and are picked up by v4 source scanning because the class is written literally in the template — do not build it dynamically.
