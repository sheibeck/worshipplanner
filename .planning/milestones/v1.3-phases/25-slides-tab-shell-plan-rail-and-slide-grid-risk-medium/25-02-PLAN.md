<objective>
Collapse video down to a slide-only concept and render it.

Phase 24 built video as a **group bed** — a clip playing across a whole group — alongside audio beds.
The user has since ruled that out: *"We won't ever have a bed video where a video plays over a whole
group of slides. We can do that for audio, but a video slide will only ever be a slide and will never
play over a group of slides."* (D-18)

So this plan does two things that MUST land together: it **deletes** the bed-video model, and it
**renders** the `VideoSlide` that 25-01 made expressible. Splitting them would leave an intermediate
commit where both media models are live — exactly the dual-model state Phase 24 existed to end.

Output: bed video gone from 7 source files, a video branch in the two assembled-slide consumers, and
five test suites updated.
</objective>
