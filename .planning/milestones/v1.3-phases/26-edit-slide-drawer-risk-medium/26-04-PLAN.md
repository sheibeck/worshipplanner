<objective>
Thread the reconciliation confirm dialog's inputs through the assembly layer — the divergence to apply,
the old and new song names, and the suppression of an already-declined divergence — and put the dialog's
warning wording in a pure, testable place.

Purpose: 26-01 gave the reconciler and the group document what they were missing; this plan carries it
the rest of the way to where a component can read it, and closes D-07's durable decline end to end. The
copy is extracted to a pure module because D-06's accepted trade-off (no diff) makes the WORDING the
entire safety mechanism — it is the only thing telling a user what they are about to lose, so it must be
unit-tested for its counts, its plural forms, and its no-media case rather than asserted through a
component render.
Output: three widened fields on the pending shape's two copies, one suppression rule, one pure copy
module addition, two extended test suites.
</objective>
