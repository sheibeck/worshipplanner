<objective>
Add the "Slide Typography" card to the Settings screen: a font-family picker, a per-family weight picker, a Small/Medium/Large size control, and a live Preview panel — with the weight list re-deriving (and snapping) on family change, on-demand loading of a previewed family, editor-gated saving, and copy from the UI-SPEC Copywriting Contract.

Purpose: This is the one place a church expresses its house font (R093 success criterion 1). It is the single write point for `authStore.settings.slideTypography`; the render sites (46-04) are pure readers. The live Preview is the card's visual focal point (UI-SPEC D2 flag) — it must show exactly what the slides will look like, which is why it binds the same `cssVarsFor` the render sites use.

Output: the Slide Typography card + its save action + tests.
</objective>
