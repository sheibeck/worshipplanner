<objective>
R106 — a per-group "Remove imported slides" action. One control on a slide group that removes
exactly its `sourceRef.kind === 'imported'` entries in a single operation, leaving auto-generated
and other manually-added entries (hand-added text/blank slides, added video) intact. Offered only
when the group has imported entries; editor-gated; draft-lock respected; mirroring the existing
per-group affordances ("+ Add background/music for this group", the congregational-reading button)
in the group-media panel of `SlideGrid.vue`.

This is NOT a general multi-select/checkbox delete mode — that was explicitly rejected
(50-CONTEXT.md LOCKED, Deferred Ideas).

Purpose: After importing a PPTX into a group, removing those slides currently means deleting them
one card at a time.

## Artifacts this plan produces
- `hasImportedEntries` computed (true when `props.group?.slides` contains an `imported` entry).
- `showRemoveImportedControl` computed (`canMutateGroup && hasImportedEntries`).
- A "Remove imported slides" button in the group-media panel and its handler `onRemoveImportedSlides`.
</objective>
