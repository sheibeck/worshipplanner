<objective>
Implement R105: a congregational scripture reading assembles to N+1 slides — a dedicated
scripture-reference slide (byte-identical to a plain scripture reference slide) followed by one
text slide per section — and section slides no longer carry the reference eyebrow (it now has its
own slide).

Approach B (LOCKED, owner 2026-08-09): emit the reference slide as an assembly-time synthetic
leading slide in BOTH paths of `src/utils/slideshowAssembler.ts`. Do NOT add a reference
`GroupSlideEntry` to `slideGroupMaterializer.ts::deriveGroupEntries`, and do NOT touch the group
rebuild/carry/signature machinery (constant `'scripture'` identity key + positional carry would
scramble per-section ids/audio — see 49-CONTEXT.md / 49-PRD.md).

Purpose: fulfil the owner's model — "a scripture slide always has a slide of the scripture
reference; making it congregational keeps that first slide as is and only adds section text
slides." Refines R097's realization (reference moves from an eyebrow on the first section to a
slide of its own).

Output: the shared reference-content helper, dual-path synthetic emission, the display-gate
change, the `isFirstSection` removal, and the extended test suites.
</objective>
