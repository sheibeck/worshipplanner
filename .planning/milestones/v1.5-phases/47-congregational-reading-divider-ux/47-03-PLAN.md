<objective>
Deliver the presentation/grid/drawer side of the ALL role and the R097 first-slide-reference behavior. R097 is a real feature to BUILD, not verify: today every congregational section slide renders both the reference and the speaker label. This plan threads an `isFirstSection` signal (from plan 01's field) through both content-resolution paths and gates the reference on it, and extends the three binary speaker-render sites to three roles.

Purpose: R097's first-slide-reference/later-slides-speaker-only outcome and the ALL role's projected legibility (R095's Leader/Congregation/All) live entirely in the render + assembly tier. The assembler stays role-agnostic (speaker passes through); the "first section" decision is computed from ordinal position.

Output: The reworked assembler paths, presenter, grid-card helpers, and drawer speaker control, with tests.
</objective>
