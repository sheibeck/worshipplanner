| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-47-02 | Tampering (data integrity) | divider slicing | low | mitigate | All segment text comes from sliceAtBoundaries + stripVerseMarkers (reused verbatim, the codebase's encoding backstop) — no hand-rolled substring/normalize.
