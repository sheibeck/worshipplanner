<objective>
Lay the type, schema, and pure-utility substrate the rest of Phase 47 builds on: the additive `'ALL'` speaker value across both slide types and the AI split contract, and the new per-verse splitter for the "Start Blank" seed. No UI in this plan — this is the foundation waves 2's editor rework (plan 02) and render/assembly work (plan 03) depend on.

Purpose: The ALL role (R095/R096/R097) and the Blank seed (R096) cannot be implemented in the editor or the render sites until the underlying unions, the AI validator, and `splitPerVerse` exist. Isolating them in wave 1 lets plans 02 and 03 run in parallel in wave 2.

Output: Widened `CongregationalSection.speaker`/`SourceRef.speaker` unions, `ScriptureSlide.isFirstSection`, a widened `SPLIT_SCHEMA`/`validateSplitResult`/`SplitSection`, a new `splitPerVerse` function, and their tests.
</objective>
