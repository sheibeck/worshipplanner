<objective>
Give the presenter the same two explicit states as the grid, in a register appropriate to a
congregation, without changing the slideshow's length or navigation by a single slide.

Implements D-15 (presenter: the same explicit states; never silently show nothing; never skip the
slide) and D-21 (visual treatment per 42-UI-SPEC.md's approved contract, including its recorded
discretionary decision to use amber rather than red here).

Purpose: 42-CONTEXT.md names a live congregation seeing a blank screen as the worst outcome in this
phase, and names skipping the slide as the second worst — skipping changes the deck's length
mid-service, so the operator's `n / m` count stops matching the plan in their hand.

Output: two new canvas states and the tests that prove nothing about navigation moved.
</objective>
