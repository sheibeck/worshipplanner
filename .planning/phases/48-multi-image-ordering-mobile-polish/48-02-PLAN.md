<objective>
Make the Slides tab usable on a phone (R099): audit-confirmed layout fixes plus touch-drag support,
without regressing the desktop reorder path or reproducing the documented SortableJS index bug.

Purpose: at 375px the fixed 260px plan rail structurally crowds out the slide grid; the drag/menu
tap targets are far under the 44px touch floor; and SortableJS is currently mouse-only. All three are
concrete blockers the 48-UI-SPEC audit found against live source.

Output (artifacts this plan produces): the responsive two-pane + rail-strip layout; the 44px hit-area
pattern on the drag handle and menu trigger; the appended SortableJS touch options on the existing
instance; and extended component tests. Two items are physical-device backstops (real touch-drag
correctness, real-thumb 44px reachability) — a human-verify checkpoint captures them.
</objective>
