<objective>
Make every output window structurally silent. Add a `suppressAudio?: boolean` prop to `SlideCanvas.vue`
that mirrors the shipped `suppressBackground` gate (forces `currentAudioUrl` to `null`, so the
`presentation-audio` wrapper — and therefore `AudioPlayer` — is never mounted), then hardcode
`:suppress-audio="true"` at all four output-tier `SlideCanvas` mount sites (two in
`FullscreenSlideOutput.vue`, which Audience and Video both delegate to; two in `ConfidenceOutputView.vue`).

Purpose: R438 under the owner's 2026-09-13 override — audio plays ONLY from the Run control window, and
"no echo" is achieved by removing every other audio owner at render time rather than by muting two of
three outputs. Plan 141-03 adds the single control-window owner; this plan makes it the only one.
Output: `SlideCanvas.suppressAudio`; four `:suppress-audio="true"` call sites; a `suppressAudio` describe
block in SlideCanvas.test.ts; stub-level `suppressAudio: true` assertions in the three output-view tests.
</objective>
