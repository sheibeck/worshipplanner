<objective>
R065 + R066, part 1 of 2 — build the inline paste region and migrate its test coverage.

**R066:** the paste stops being a Teleported modal and becomes an inline region that swaps into
`SongLyricEditor`'s own 480px panel in place.
**R065:** a paste with detected sections but no CCLI number renders a warning card and **blocks**
`Replace lyrics` — with an override checkbox that is **always** available so the paste stays
completable in every case.

This plan creates the new component and its tests standing alone. Plan 04 (wave 2) hosts it in
`SongLyricEditor.vue` and deletes the modal. Splitting this way keeps each plan inside its context
budget and keeps tests landing with the code they cover.

Purpose: one paste surface, no silent acceptance of an uncredited song, and no route to a dead end
where a user holds pasted text they cannot save.

Output: `src/components/LyricPasteRegion.vue` and `src/components/__tests__/LyricPasteRegion.test.ts`.

## File-fate decision (Claude's Discretion, resolved here)

35-UI-SPEC.md §4 leaves `LyricPasteDialog.vue`'s post-conversion fate to the plan, and
35-RESEARCH.md Open Question 2 recommends the lower-churn option. **Decision: a separate child
component, renamed `LyricPasteRegion.vue`, mounted inline with no Teleport, no backdrop, no
`fixed`/`z-50` positioning and no `max-h-[85vh]`.** The test file can keep mounting one component
directly, which is what makes the 9 parsing/save tests movable rather than rewritable.

**The component is multi-root:** it renders the `lyrics-paste-header` row and the `paste-region`
body as two sibling root elements. Vue 3 fragments render children directly into the parent DOM, so
both remain direct children of `SongLyricEditor`'s `flex h-full flex-col` root and the rendered DOM
matches 35-UI-SPEC.md §4 exactly. The reason for folding the header in rather than leaving it in the
host: the `‹ Back to sections` guard reads `rawText`, and `rawText` lives here. Splitting them would
force a `defineExpose`/ref dance across a component boundary for no benefit.

**Emit names stay `close` and `saved`**, identical to `LyricPasteDialog.vue`'s today. That is a
deliberate churn-avoidance choice: three of the nine migrated tests assert on those emit names, and
renaming them would turn a verbatim move into an edit.
</objective>
