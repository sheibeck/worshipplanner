| T-140-12 | Information disclosure | stale cross-org vamps surfacing in the Vamps tab after a sidebar church switch | high | mitigate | The new `vampStore` watch on `authStore.orgId` ({immediate:true}) unsubscribes + re-subscribes on switch (local half); the global `resetOrgScopedStores()` teardown was registered by 140-01 (verified by its orgScopedStores test). Prevents Church-A vamps rendering under Church B. |

Server-side authority (140-02) is the real gate regardless of UI reachability. Documented residual, not a phase blocker. |
| T-140-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | Zero new dependencies — pure Vue components + existing store/composable imports (RESEARCH Package Legitimacy Audit — "none"). No package-legitimacy checkpoint required. |
