<objective>
Close Phase 31: verify the lock end-to-end by hand, and hand the rules deployment to the
owner (D-16).

**★ Why this plan exists at all.** `31-RESEARCH.md` established three facts that combine
into a trap:
1. `firestore.rules` does **not** ship with the app bundle — it deploys separately.
2. This repo has **no CI** (`.github/workflows` does not exist).
3. `src/rules.test.ts` is **excluded** from the default vitest run (`vite.config.ts:85-86`).

So a broken or undeployed lock ships green twice over: `npx vitest run` says nothing about
it, and `npm run build` says nothing about it. Without this plan, the most likely outcome of
Phase 31 is a lock that passes every automated check and does not exist in production.
</objective>

## Task 3 — BLOCKING human-verify checkpoint

as passed, until they respond.

**Lock behaviour**
1. Open a Draft service. Everything is editable as before — Service Order, Slides, Roles.
2. Click **Mark as Planned**. Expect: one lock banner appears; Service Order rows become
   plain text with no drag handles, no Add item, no pickers; the Slides tab offers no Add
   slide / Import / drag / drawer edits; Roles shows names with no checkboxes.
3. Switch between all three tabs. Expect: exactly ONE banner, and it does not re-announce
   per tab.
4. Confirm **Export to PC**, **Present**, **Print** and **Share** all still work while
   locked.
5. **★ Confirm no empty state tells you to do something you cannot do** — check an empty
   service section, the slide grid, the plan rail, and Roles.

**Reopen**
6. Click **Reopen for editing** on the Planned service. Expect: one click, no dialog,
   editing restored.
7. **★ Confirm drag-and-drop works again immediately, with no page reload.** This is the
   Sortable-teardown regression — if the tab is undraggable after reopen, the fix broke it.
8. Export a service to Planning Center, then Reopen it. Expect: a confirm dialog carrying
   the Planning Center warning. Cancel it — status must be unchanged. Confirm it — status
   returns to Draft.
9. Re-export that reopened service. Expect: it can still target the SAME Planning Center
   plan (D-11). This is the case the naive rule denied.

**Enforcement beyond the UI**
