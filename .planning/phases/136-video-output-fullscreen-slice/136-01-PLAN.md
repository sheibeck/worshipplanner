<objective>
Widen the monitor output-role model to include a third role, `video`, and let Monitor Setup
assign it — the R424 "role + setup" half of the Video output slice. Purely additive to the v2.9
N-assignment model: the `MonitorRole` enum grows one member, the untrusted-localStorage validator
widens to accept it, and the per-monitor role picker offers Video as an opt-in third choice. The
≥1-Audience Save gate is deliberately UNCHANGED — Video is optional, exactly like Confidence.

Purpose: establish the type + persistence + authoring surface that Plan 02's VideoOutputView,
route, and launch depend on. Without the enum + validator widening here, a saved `video`
assignment would be silently dropped by `isValidMapping` (the T-91-01 untrusted-read guard) and
could never launch.
Output: `MonitorRole` includes `video`; `isValidMapping` accepts it; MonitorCard + MonitorSetupView
offer Video as a third role; unit + component tests cover the new role.
</objective>
