## Flagged planner assumption (UI-SPEC E3 `long-text`, unresolved)

`33-UI-SPEC.md`'s E3 `long-text` row is the one **unresolved** UI consideration in this phase: the
uploaded file's ORIGINAL filename is user-controlled (unlike every other string in the table, which
is a fixed enum), `truncate` is specified for the display span, but no maximum-length pre-upload
validation is. Research confirmed `useMediaUpload.ts`'s `sanitizeFileName` strips disallowed
characters and imposes **no** maximum length.

**Assumption this plan proceeds under: there is no upload-time filename length cap, and none is
added.** Display-side `truncate` is the whole mitigation. Do not silently introduce a cap — if one is
wanted, it is a decision to raise, not to absorb.
