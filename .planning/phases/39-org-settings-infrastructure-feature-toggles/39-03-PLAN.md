<objective>
Add both feature toggles to the Settings screen — an AI Features section that explains before it
offers the switch, and a Planning Center enable toggle inside the existing Planning Center
Integration section — and move every settings write on this screen onto concurrency-safe dot-path
leaf keys.

Purpose: this is the first user-visible settings surface in v1.5 and the write half of the
`vwModeEnabled` migration. ROADMAP success criteria 2 and 3 both begin "In Settings, a church can
turn ... off," and this plan is where that becomes true.

Output: two new toggle blocks in `SettingsView.vue`, three dot-path save handlers, and the R073
payload-shape plus R089 credential-retention assertions in `SettingsView.test.ts`.
