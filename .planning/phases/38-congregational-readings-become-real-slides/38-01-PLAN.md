<objective>
Land the two states a scripture slide group can be in — **Reference** (payload-free, mirrored
from the slot, unchanged from today) and **Congregational** (N entries, one per section,
detached from slot-driven re-derivation) — in the pure layer: types, the congregational
predicate, the derivation, the change signature, the rebuild state machine, and the assembler's
per-entry resolution.

Implements D1 (D-01 in decision-coverage terms) — congregational conversion DETACHES the group —
and the storage half of D2 (D-02) — a detached section entry owns its own words, with no
write-back path to the slot's reading.

Purpose: this is the whole mechanism behind roadmap criteria 1, 4 and 5. Everything after this
plan is presentation and editing surface.
Output: a congregational scripture item produces one slide per section, each independently
stored; a deleted section slide stays deleted; an existing Phase-34 service upgrades itself.
</objective>

`rebuildScriptureGroup`: this is where the two states live. In order:

   deleted every section slide gets an empty group that stays empty until the scripture itself
   changes.
