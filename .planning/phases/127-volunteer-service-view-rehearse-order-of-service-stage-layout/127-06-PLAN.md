| T-127-09 | Elevation of Privilege | standalone volunteer view | high | mitigate | Render a standalone read-only view only — never ServiceEditorView or any editing/messaging chrome; the route keeps isVolunteerRoute meta so a zero-membership volunteer session is exempted from the org-selection gate but gains no editor surface. |
| T-127-02 | Spoofing | orgId path | high | mitigate | Delegate orgId resolution entirely to useVolunteerServiceDoc (store-sourced, never a route input); the view passes only serviceId. |
