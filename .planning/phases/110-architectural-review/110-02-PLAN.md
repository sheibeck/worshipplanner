<severity_rubric>
Per 110-CONTEXT.md (owner-accepted 2026-09-02), classify every finding:
- Critical — data loss, cross-tenant data leak, or auth bypass.
- High — a correctness bug or multi-tenant isolation weakness likely to bite under real use.
- Medium — maintainability/coupling risk, or a latent bug that needs specific conditions.
- Low — nits / style.
Critical + High are remediated in Phase 111; Medium + Low are triaged to backlog. Record the severity
explicitly on every finding so the consolidation plan can separate the two groups unambiguously. (Most
boundary/coupling/data-flow findings will land Medium/Low; escalate to High/Critical only where a
boundary break or coupling defect can actually cause a correctness bug or data loss.)
</severity_rubric>
