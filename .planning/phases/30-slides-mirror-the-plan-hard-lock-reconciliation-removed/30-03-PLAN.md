<objective>
Make song groups read-only in the Slides tab (R054), and finish R047's rendering ripple in
the slide drawer.

Purpose: songs are canonical. Per-service slide edits reintroduce the wrong-slides-at-
rehearsal failure the canonical-song guarantee exists to prevent — the owner's words were
"this makes it so someone can't mess with songs." A song's slides are edited from the Song
Lyrics screen and nowhere else.

The riskiest part of this plan is a test fixture, not the feature. `mountDrawer()` in
`EditSlideDrawer.test.ts` hardcodes a SONG plan item and is called 93 times, 92 of them
without an override — most asserting that the very controls this plan removes ARE present.
Gating the drawer before repointing that default would flip roughly 92 passing assertions
in one commit and bury the real signal. Task 1 repoints the default FIRST, on its own, with
the suite green before any production change. The factory already spreads its overrides
last, so this is a one-line change plus a handful of context-line fixups — not a
92-call-site rewrite.

Output: a Slides tab where a planner can arrange songs but not edit them, and where the
lock explains itself.
</objective>
