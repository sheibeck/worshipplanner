## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-46-03 | Tampering | `cssVarsFor` / `snapWeight` reading `slideTypography` from Firestore | medium | mitigate | `cssVarsFor` defensively falls back to Inter/400/md when the stored family is not a `SLIDE_FONTS` key, the weight is not in the family ramp, or the scale is not sm\|md\|lg — the value written into `--slide-font-family` and the `document.fonts.load()` string is therefore always drawn from the curated set, never free text (ASVS V5). |
| T-46-04 | Denial of Service | `waitForSlideFont` gate | low | mitigate | `Promise.race` against `FONT_LOAD_TIMEOUT_MS` guarantees the gate always settles — a stalled/missing font asset degrades to "render anyway", never a hung loading screen. |
</threat_model>
