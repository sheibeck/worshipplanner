<objective>
Close the two things the earlier plans could not: the team-facing surfaces, and the proof.

First, the printed service order and the shared service view currently dispatch on slot kind through
`v-else-if` chains with no trailing arm. An ANNOUNCEMENTS or MISC slot renders as literally nothing
on both — a planner adds an item and it silently vanishes from the artifact the team actually reads.
That is the same silent-omission failure class R085 exists to close, on two more surfaces.

Second, R084's hard half. Plan 03 removed the Hymn chip; asserting that a chip is gone proves the
easy half. This plan proves the half that matters — that a church's existing Hymn items still render,
print, present and export exactly as before — and it proves that the exhaustiveness backstop plan 02
built actually fires, by making it fire.

Purpose: no item type that a planner can create may be invisible on the surfaces the team reads, and
no retired type's existing content may have degraded without anyone noticing.
Output: complete print and share coverage for the new kinds, a cross-surface HYMN regression suite,
and a demonstrated compile-time guard.
</objective>

## Scope note — this plan extends the UI-SPEC's stated surface, deliberately and on the record

43-UI-SPEC.md scopes itself to three surfaces inside `src/views/ServiceEditorView.vue`. It does not
cover print or share, because it was written as an editor design contract. Leaving those two surfaces
untouched would mean an ANNOUNCEMENTS item renders as nothing at all on the printed order and on the
shared link. That is not a design choice the UI-SPEC made; it is a consequence of a chain of
`v-else-if` branches with no trailing arm. This plan therefore extends past the UI-SPEC's stated
surface, using each of those two files' own existing conventions rather than importing the editor's,
and the extension is recorded here so it is a disclosed decision rather than an unexplained diff.
