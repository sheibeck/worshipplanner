142-UI-SPEC.md §5 specifies: `VampPicker` gains `allowUnattached` so the group popover can pick a vamp with

no-MP3 vamp assignable + amber warning, inline picker, Track row with Replace/Remove.

audio state correctly on its own. The control stays emit-only — `SlideGrid.vue` remains the sole Firestore
write owner (PATTERNS.md).

with a rewritten test suite; three small interim touch-ups in `SlideGrid.test.ts` so the grid's suite stays
green until Plan 04 rewrites its group-media blocks.
