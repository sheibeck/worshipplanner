<objective>
Remove the per-plan-item media-attachment control from the Service Order tab and delete the component it
mounted, which nothing else imports.

media, and Phase 25-06's group-music control on the Slides tab already superseded this surface — the
phase context records it as "largely superseded", and this plan finishes that.
Output: A Service Order tab whose plan-item rows carry only service-structure controls, and one fewer
dead component on disk.
