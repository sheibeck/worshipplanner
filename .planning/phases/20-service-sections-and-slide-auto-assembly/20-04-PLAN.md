<objective>
Deliver the Phase 20 user-facing surface (R018, R007 UI): section headers/dividers grouping the
service slot list, a per-slot section-assignment control, and an inline SlideshowPreview panel that
renders the live assembled slideshow from `useSlideshowAssembly` and updates automatically as the user
reorders/adds/removes elements. Existing flat, section-less services must keep rendering exactly as
today (no section headers, no migration). This plan also closes the Phase 19 carryover gap: manually
overridden scripture slides get a visible visual distinction (roadmap invited folding it into Phase 20
UI polish).

Purpose: Make the assembly engine and composable visible and usable for a non-technical volunteer —
the acceptance-criteria payoff ("add songs and scripture, see the slideshow auto-assemble; reorder and
watch slides follow"). SortableJS stays a single flat sortable list (MEM008 DOM-revert pattern
preserved) — sections are visual grouping only, not separate drag containers.

Output: new SlideshowPreview.vue (+ test), ServiceEditorView section UI + preview mount, and the
ScriptureSlideEditor override-visual fix. Ends with a human-verify checkpoint.
</objective>
