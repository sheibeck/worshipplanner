<objective>
Add the per-item Banner/Full-screen authoring choice for the Video output (R425): the optional
`videoOutput` schema field on `MediaAttachableSlot`, a new `SlotVideoOutputControl.vue` two-segment
pill toggle, and the relay/persistence wiring that rides the existing autosave — a byte-for-byte mirror
of the `loop` field precedent.

Purpose: Give an editor an authoring surface to mark a specific slide item as a lower-third Banner on the
Video output. Absent/`fullscreen` = full-screen (the locked default, Phase 136 behavior). This plan ships
the schema + control + persistence; the actual banner RENDER is Plan 02 (which type-depends on this field).

Output: `videoOutput` field on the base slot interface, `SlotVideoOutputControl.vue` + its test, and the
SlideGrid → SlidesTab → ServiceEditorView relay that writes `slot.videoOutput` through the single
`useAutoSave` deep-watch.
</objective>
