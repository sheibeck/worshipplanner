<objective>
Lay the security + schema foundation for editor presence: a new org-scoped
`organizations/{orgId}/services/{serviceId}/presence/{uid}` subcollection, its
firestore.rules read/write/delete block (the get/list-split, own-doc-only idiom),
and the ALLOW/DENY rules tests that prove no cross-tenant read leak and no
other-user write. This lands FIRST — Plan 02 (the composable) imports the schema
type from `src/utils/presence.ts` and depends on these rules to read/write live.

Purpose: The presence subcollection is a new per-service read/write surface. Its
single load-bearing risk is the read rule staying org-scoped (never a smaller
replay of the v2.8 SEC-S-01 cross-tenant leak) and the write staying own-doc-only.
Output: `src/utils/presence.ts`, the firestore.rules presence match block, and a
comprehensive presence describe block in `src/rules.test.ts`.
</objective>
