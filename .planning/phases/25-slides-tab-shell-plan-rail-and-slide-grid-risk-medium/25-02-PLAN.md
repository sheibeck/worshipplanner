    - The video-slide player is mounted under a distinct test id, so bed-video assertions and video-slide assertions never collide.
    - A video slide whose group also has a bed video renders only the video slide's own player — the bed block does not additionally render on that slide.

    - A video slide whose autoplay is hard-blocked shows the existing tap-to-play video affordance; one that fell back to muted playback shows the existing muted chip.

  <action>
Add `video` to the `CardKind` union so `cardKind()` narrows the new content kind explicitly rather than
letting it fall out of the branch chain.

Add a video branch to the slide-canvas chain, placed after the image branch, rendering a chromeless
`VideoPlayer` bound to the slide's own source field (the one 25-01 named distinctly from the bed
carrier). Give this block its own `data-testid` — the bed block's `presentation-video` id must keep
meaning "bed video" — and size it like the image branch does, constrained to the viewport rather than
the small centred wrapper the bed block uses, since here the video IS the slide.

Bind this player to the SAME `videoRef` template ref the bed block uses. The two blocks are mutually
exclusive (see the suppression below), so only one is ever mounted, and reusing the ref means
`playCurrentMedia`, `pauseCurrentMedia`, `onMediaError`, `onVideoAutoplayBlocked`, the tap-to-play
affordance and the muted chip all work for video slides with no new driving code and no second
lifecycle to keep in sync. Wire the same three event handlers the bed player uses.

Key this player per slide, matching the non-bed half of the existing `currentVideoKey` formula: a video
slide is per-slide media by definition and must remount on every slide change (Phase 23's WR-02
guarantee against leaked muted state). Do not route it through the group-continuity branch of that
computed — that branch exists for beds, which deliberately survive slide transitions within a group.

Add the suppression: the existing bed-video block gains one more condition so it does not render when
the current slide is itself a video slide. Without it, a group carrying both a bed video and a video
slide would mount two players and play two soundtracks at once. Add nothing else to that block — its
test id, its wrapper classes, its key and its handlers stay exactly as they are.

Leave `bodyIsCaption` alone. It demotes a slide's own TEXT to caption scale when a bed video is
present; a video slide has no text of its own, so the computed is irrelevant on it and changing it
would alter behaviour for existing lyric and scripture slides.

Extend `src/components/__tests__/PresentationViewer.test.ts` with the behaviours above. Include an
explicit regression test asserting a non-video slide in a bed-video group still renders under the
existing bed test id.
  </action>
