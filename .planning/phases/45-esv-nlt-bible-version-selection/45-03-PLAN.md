<objective>
Build the per-slide translation-provenance foundation that makes R092 true by construction: the `translationSource: 'ESV' | 'NLT'` field on the slide/source types, the two pure helpers `scriptureAttribution()` (R091) and `resolveTranslationSource()` (R092 field-less fallback), and the assembly threading that carries a stamped value from `CongregationalSection` -> `SourceRef` -> `ScriptureSlide` without ever re-deriving it.

This plan is pure/additive types, helpers, and passthrough wiring — no UI, no fetch, no component. The stamping-at-fetch and the render-site consumption live in 45-04; this plan supplies the field, the helpers, and the plumbing they depend on.

The single most important line in the whole phase lives here: `resolveTranslationSource()` returns `slide.translationSource ?? 'ESV'` and MUST NEVER read `authStore`/`OrgSettings` — reading the current setting would silently violate "changing the setting never retroactively alters existing slides" (45-RESEARCH.md § Don't Hand-Roll #4, the failure is silent with no type or test signal unless explicitly asserted).

Purpose: Establish the provenance field + helpers + threading so R091 attribution and R092 immutability are guaranteed at the data layer.
Output: the field on 3 types, 2 helpers in scripture.ts, materializer + assembler threading, and tests proving the R092 invariant and the ESV fallback.
</objective>
